# M3 Story Construction independent ACCEPT

ACCEPT is a new read-only session/worktree fixed to an exact candidate commit/tree. It does not trust Builder explanations and independently recomputes canonical JSON, base/proposed Canon hashes, governance Merkle root, receipt payload hash, source/supersession bindings, route identities, schemas, package exclusion, deterministic builds, and M2 preservation.

Activation additionally requires a single-use, non-transferable Owner approval whose complete identity tuple matches the candidate. Any mismatch, missing T1/T2/C1 approval, open high-severity defect, or modified candidate is FAIL/BLOCKED. ACCEPT never rewrites the candidate.
