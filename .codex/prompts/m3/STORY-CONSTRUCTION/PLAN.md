# M3 Story Construction PLAN

PLAN alone may define a future Story Construction batch, its exact paths, dependencies, approvals, schemas, orders, tests, and route request. It must preserve R2 precedence and M2 lifecycle, reject missing Canon instead of inventing it, and keep global active WIP within the Owner-approved limit.

PLAN does not modify content, invoke a worker, create a candidate, or approve T1/T2/C1. It outputs a closed Task Envelope and explicit stop conditions.
