# Reveal and Epistemic Model

```text
AUTHORITY_SCOPE = CONSTRUCTION_GOVERNANCE_ONLY
R2_CANON_PRECEDENCE = HIGHER
```

The model keeps these concepts distinct: `Truth`, `Observation`, `Claim`, `PublicClaim`, `Evidence`, `Hypothesis`, `Knowledge`, `Belief`, `DeceptionIntent`, `SourceReliability`, and `Confidence`. None may be silently promoted into another.

A C1 Reveal Contract states what information a participant may receive, when, through which channel, with what reliability, ambiguity, and confidence. Related contracts form a Reveal Family and require Owner approval. An Epistemic Signature is the canonical comparison surface for player cognition; it is not Canon itself.

Validation covers text reveals, visual reveals, same-scene and cross-scene composition, shared information, repeat/loop variants, and equivalent accessible channels. Text and visual assets have no authority over the Canon Registry and cannot collectively reveal more than the approved contract.
