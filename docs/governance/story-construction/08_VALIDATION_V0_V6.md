# Validation V0–V6

```text
AUTHORITY_SCOPE = CONSTRUCTION_GOVERNANCE_ONLY
R2_CANON_PRECEDENCE = HIGHER
```

- V0: Schema and format.
- V1: references, versions, hashes, and provenance.
- V2: Canon, rule, and causal consistency.
- V3: NPC cognition, Reveal, and epistemic consistency.
- V4: route, state, loop, failure, retreat, and remediation reachability.
- V5: cross-modal semantic consistency and accessibility equivalence.
- V6: approval, rights, candidate binding, release, and supply-chain integrity.

Defect severities are `BLOCKER`, `CRITICAL`, `MAJOR`, `MINOR`, and `COSMETIC`. Any open BLOCKER, CRITICAL, or MAJOR prevents Region Acceptance. A worker self-check is evidence only and cannot be the final oracle.
